Field note 01

Venus runs backwards

Venus rotates east-to-west, so its Sun rises in the west. Its day is longer than its year: 243 Earth days of rotation against 225 days of orbit.

Field note 02

Jupiter does most of the sweeping

With 2.5 times the mass of every other planet combined, Jupiter shapes asteroid belts and deflects or captures many incoming objects. Its Great Red Spot has been observed for at least 190 years.

Jupiter’s known moons: 95

Field note 03

Neptune was found by mathematics

Astronomers predicted Neptune’s position from irregularities in Uranus’s orbit. It was observed within about a degree of the prediction in 1846.
